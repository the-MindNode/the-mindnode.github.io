MindNode

Elegant mind mapping for Mac. MindNode is a powerful, intuitive tool for brainstorming and organizing ideas. Its clutter-free interface and infinite canvas let you focus on connecting thoughts visually, with seamless iCloud sync keeping your maps everywhere you need them.

Visual Idea Capture

Nodes with titles, notes, images, tasks, and links. Quick Entry for instant capture. Branches and connections reveal relationships at a glance.

Flexible Layouts

Top-down or horizontal layouts. Two branch shapes, multiple node shapes. Outline view with search for structured navigation.

Customization

Multiple themes, unlimited personal themes, and extensive styling options. Tailor every element to match your vision.

Seamless Integration

Full screen mode for focus. Export to text, PDF, image, or outline. Share tasks to Reminders, Things, or OmniFocus. myMindNode for easy web sharing.

How It Works

1. Start with Quick Entry

Capture ideas instantly. Add titles, notes, images, tasks, or links to any node—no friction, just flow.

2. Build Connections

Drag branches to show relationships. Switch between top-down, horizontal layouts, or outline view as your map evolves.

3. Customize and Refine

Apply themes, tweak shapes and styles. Search, fold, or expand for clarity on complex projects.

4. Share and Export

Sync via iCloud. Export formats or integrate with task apps. Use myMindNode for collaborative web links.
